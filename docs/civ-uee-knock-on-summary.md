# CIV (Electrotechnology) — UEE Training Package Knock-On Risk

## What's happening
The national UEE Electrotechnology Training Package is currently under a full review — 74 qualifications, 552 units, 75 skill sets — led by Powering Skills Organisation. Draft consultation opens May 2026.

## How the proposed CIV is exposed
The 22697VIC CIV, packaged with an Electrotechnology-stream elective set, requires exactly 6 units from that stream. Of those 6, **5 are national UEE units, not Victorian-specific**:

| Unit | Content |
|---|---|
| UEECD0007 | WHS/OHS regulations |
| UEECD0019 | Fabricate/assemble utilities components |
| UEECD0046 | Solve problems in single-path circuits |
| UEERL0003 | In-service safety testing |
| UEECD0052 | Industry fundamentals — energy sector equipment |

There is **no spare capacity** in this stream — exactly 6 slots, exactly 6 units selected. If any of these are superseded, merged, or deleted as part of the review, the qualification's packaging rules can't absorb it as-is.

## Why UEECD0007 specifically is a flashpoint
This unit alone sits inside roughly 170 qualifications across 11 training packages nationally. Units with that much cross-package reach have a documented history of being contested and revised whenever a package review touches them — it's not a fringe unit that's unlikely to move.

## The timing problem
- CIV packaging finalised for 2026 delivery at Joondalup
- Cohort starts Semester 1, 2026
- UEE draft consultation opens May 2026 — mid-way through the first delivery period
- If any of the 5 units are flagged for change, a formal packaging variation is required to keep delivering the CIV as currently structured — potentially while a cohort is partway through it

## What this doesn't affect
The same qualification code (22697VIC) can also be packaged with a **Robotics-stream** elective set instead. That packaging draws only on Victorian-coded and Manufacturing-package (MEM) units — none from the UEE package — so it carries none of this exposure. The risk is specific to the Electrotechnology-stream packaging, not the qualification as a whole.
